Teaching

At Northeastern University, I have led recitation sections of Principles of Macroeconomics in five terms since Summer 2024, supporting a 400-student lecture course. I have also been a teaching assistant for five other courses.

My teaching interests include Principles of Macroeconomics, Principles of Microeconomics, Microeconomic Theory, Statistics, and Econometrics, as well as Health Economics and Labor Economics.

Recitations

Recitation for Principles of Macroeconomics ECON 1125

Fall 2026, Spring 2026, Spring 2025, Fall 2024, and Summer 2024. Lectures taught by Dr. Jill Dupree and Dr. Peter Simon.

  • Lead four recitation sections per term (two in Spring 2026) for ECON 1115, a 400-student lecture course.
  • Designed and teach a weekly discussion that connects macroeconomic theory to current news, data, and applications.
  • Supervise and grade a semester-long project in which groups of two or three students build five case studies of one country’s macroeconomic performance.
  • Hold office hours and grade problem sets, discussion boards, and exams.
